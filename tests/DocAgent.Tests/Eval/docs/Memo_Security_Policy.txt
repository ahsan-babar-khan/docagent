MEMO – Information security policy update

To: all staff. Project: Internal.
From 1 January 2027, multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every system that holds customer data.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
Customer documents may not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.
Security incidents must be reported to security@northwind.example within 24 hours.
The policy owner is the Chief Information Security Officer, Anna Laine.
